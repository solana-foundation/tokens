The v2 asset surface is chain-native: the same curated assets as v1, with their tokens on Solana,
Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Polygon and BNB. This page is the model; the routes are on
[Chains](/v2/endpoints/chains), [Assets](/v2/endpoints/assets) and
[Variants](/v2/endpoints/variants), and the TypeScript types are in
[Shared shapes](/v2/concepts/shared-shapes).

## Two nouns

- An **asset** is the curated, chain-agnostic thing: `usd`, `bitcoin`, `tesla`. It lives under
  `GET /v2/assets` and `GET /v2/assets/{assetId}`.
- A **variant** is one token on one chain that represents an asset. It has exactly one identifier,
  the key `<chain>:<address>` (`solana:EPjF…`, `base:0x8335…`), and lives under
  `GET /v2/variants/{chain}/{address}` and the batch `GET /v2/variants?keys=`.

Every v2 asset is curated. There are no synthetic per-mint assets: an address-shaped `{assetId}`
is `400 invalid_asset_id`, and a token the registry does not know is `404 variant_not_found` (or
`missing[]` in a batch). For unregistered Solana tokens use
[token lists](/v2/concepts/token-lists) or [search](/v2/concepts/risk-aware-search).

## Chains and CAIP-2 ids

The chain list is compiled into the API, so adding a chain is a release, never a data change.
Cache `GET /v2/chains` freely.

| `id` | `caip2` | Family | Explorer |
|---|---|---|---|
| `solana` | `solana:5eykt4UsFv8P8NJdTREpY1vzqKqZKvdp` | `svm` | Solana Explorer |
| `ethereum` | `eip155:1` | `evm` | Etherscan |
| `base` | `eip155:8453` | `evm` | Basescan |
| `arbitrum` | `eip155:42161` | `evm` | Arbiscan |
| `polygon` | `eip155:137` | `evm` | Polygonscan |
| `bnb` | `eip155:56` | `evm` | BscScan |

The `id` column is the `chain` value on every variant and the value every `chain=` param and
`<chain>:<address>` key accepts. Provider-side slugs (`polygon-pos`, `arbitrum-one`, `bsc`) are
not accepted and return `400` with `details.code: "invalid_chain"`. Each chain also publishes a
`caip2` id ([CAIP-2](https://chainagnostic.org/CAIPs/caip-2)), an `evmChainId` (`null` for
Solana) and an explorer URL template with an `{address}` placeholder.

## Variant keys

A `0x` address is the same string on every EVM chain, so no v2 input is a bare address: the chain
is a path segment (`/v2/variants/{chain}/{address}`) or the key prefix (`keys=base:0x…`).

- **EVM** addresses are `0x` plus 40 hex characters. They are accepted in any casing and always
  returned **lowercase**. Compare keys, not checksummed strings.
- **Solana** addresses are case-sensitive base58, as in v1.
- A malformed address is `400 invalid_address` with `details.expected` set to `base58` or
  `0x40hex`.

## Capabilities per chain

`GET /v2/chains` publishes a capability flag set per chain. The flags are the contract for what
the rest of the surface can return for a variant on that chain; gate your UI on them, not on
`chain === 'solana'`.

| Flag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `markets` | Per-variant market snapshot (price, liquidity, volume) |
| `dexMarkets` | Per-pool DEX market lists |
| `ohlcv` | Candles |
| `risk` | Risk summary and details; also required for `pegHealth` |
| `executionQuality` | Fill-quality snapshots |
| `swap` | Swap and trade links, execution routing; defines the "executable" pool below |
| `firstPartyLogos` | tokens.xyz-hosted logo mirror |

Solana has every capability. The EVM chains have `markets` only: no OHLCV, risk, DEX market
lists, execution quality, swap routing or first-party logos. When an EVM capability is switched on
it flips in `GET /v2/chains` first, so a client that gates on the flags needs no code change.

What this means for variants: `market` is populated on every chain where a snapshot exists.
Execution quality and peg health are opt-in on the asset detail
(`GET /v2/assets/{assetId}?include=executionQuality,pegHealth`, plus `preStocks` for Solana
PreStocks tokens) and are `null` on chains without the `executionQuality` or `risk` capability.
OHLCV, risk, DEX market lists and swap routing are not exposed on v2 variants; use the v1 asset
routes for Solana mints.

## All chains by default

An asset's `chains`, per-chain `chainStats`, cross-chain `aggregate`, `stats`, `primaryVariant`
and `primaryVariantByChain` are always computed from its **full** variant set.

`chain=` narrows what is **listed**, never what is **summed**. On `GET /v2/assets` it keeps only
assets with at least one variant on that chain and, with `include=variants`, lists only that
chain's variants. On `GET /v2/assets/{assetId}/variants` it filters the rows. The numbers above do
not change, and each variant's `rank` stays its liquidity rank within the whole asset across
chains.

## Per-chain stats, aggregate and reference market

- `chainStats.<chain>` is that chain's on-chain view. Liquidity, 24h volume and 24h trade count
  are summed over every variant on the chain (pools are distinct). Market cap, FDV and
  circulating supply are summed only over variants whose `issuance` is `native`: a bridged or
  wrapped token is a claim on supply issued elsewhere, and `excludedFromSupplyCount` tells you how
  many were left out. `chainStats.solana` is the number v1 reported.
- `aggregate` is the sum of `chainStats` across chains, plus `chainCount` and
  `excludedFromSupplyCount`.
- `referenceMarket` is the off-chain reference (v1's `canonicalMarket`): CoinGecko for crypto
  (`source: "coingecko"`), the stock benchmark for mapped public equities (`source: "stock"`), the
  PreStocks mark for tokenized pre-IPO equities (`source: "prestocks"`). Keep a default branch in
  any exhaustive `switch (source)`.
- `stats` is the headline view: price, market cap and supply come from `referenceMarket` when
  there is one, with on-chain liquidity and volume from `aggregate`. `volume30dUSD` is a Solana
  rolling 30-day figure and `null` elsewhere.
- Liquidity is provider-reported per chain, so treat cross-chain comparisons as approximate.

## Primary variant and `primaryScope`

`primaryVariant` is the variant to trade. Selection has two steps: pick a candidate pool, then
rank inside it.

With the default `primaryScope=executable`, candidates are drawn from chains whose `swap`
capability is on (Solana), taking the first non-empty pool:

1. `executable_active` — actively traded variants on executable chains
2. `active` — actively traded variants on any chain
3. `executable` — any variant on an executable chain
4. `all` — everything

"Actively traded" means at least $100 of 24h volume and 5 trades. `primaryScope=global` skips the
executable pools and gives the cross-chain liquidity winner.

Inside the pool the applied `primaryVariantStrategy` ranks: `liquidity` by default,
`stock_redeemability` for equities (redeemable tiers first, then liquidity), or
`execution_quality`. A variant with no market data never outranks one that trades, and
`compromised` or `blocked` variants are never chosen. `primaryVariantSelection` reports the pool
and the deciding rule so the choice is reproducible, and `primaryVariantByChain` runs the same
ranking inside each chain.

## Advisories

Advisories are keyed by Solana mint, so EVM variants always carry `advisory: null`. An asset's
`advisories[]` lists every flagged variant, most severe first, including `blocked` siblings that
list surfaces hide.

On the v2 asset surface: `GET /v2/assets` omits `blocked` variants from every row and drops an
asset whose variants are all blocked; `GET /v2/assets/{assetId}`, the `/variants` sub-route and
the variant routes serve them with the advisory attached. The long form is in
[Advisories](/v1/concepts/advisories).
